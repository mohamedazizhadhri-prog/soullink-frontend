Abstract

This project was carried out as part of a Bachelor’s degree in Business Information
Systems. It focuses on the design and development of a web application using the MERN
stack to manage the internal recruitment of interns at Orange Tunisie – Network and
Services Division. The main goal is to digitize the recruitment process by centralizing
internship sessions, candidate submissions, their automated sorting based on predefined
criteria, as well as handling complaints. The project was developed using the Scrum
agile methodology, promoting iterative development and continuous improvement.

Keywords : MERN Stack, Scrum, Recruitment, Web application, Orange Tunisie.

R´esum´e

Ce projet a ´et´e r´ealis´e dans le cadre de l’obtention d’une licence en Business Information
System. Il porte sur la conception et le d´eveloppement d’une application web bas´ee sur
la pile MERN, destin´ee `a g´erer le recrutement interne des stagiaires au sein d’Orange
Tunisie – Direction R´eseaux et Services. L’objectif principal est de num´eriser le pro-
cessus de recrutement `a travers la centralisation des sessions de stage, la soumission
des candidatures, leur tri automatis´e selon des crit`eres d´efinis, ainsi que le traitement
des r´eclamations. Le d´eveloppement a ´et´e men´e selon la m´ethodologie agile Scrum,
favorisant une ´evolution it´erative et une am´elioration continue.
